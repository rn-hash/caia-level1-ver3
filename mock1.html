Q001 · L1-1.1.1-002

Which of the following is least likely to be considered a typical characteristic of alternative investments?
  • . Potentially high levels of concentration in a single position or strategy.
  • . Lower levels of liquidity compared with traditional publicly traded securities.
  • . The use of leverage, short selling, and derivatives.
  • . Mandatory daily pricing and continuous secondary-market trading.
正解: D
🎯 秒殺テク
- 武器2「least likely問題は『明らかに正しい・当たり前の選択肢』が答え」: Dの「日次評価・継続的な流通市場取引が必須」は伝統的投資の常識であって、代替投資には当てはまらない→これが答え - A（高集中）B（低流動性）C（レバレッジ・空売り・デリバティブ）は代替投資の典型特徴そのもの→正しいので絶対に選ばない - 代替投資＝「流動性が低く、評価頻度が低い（四半期・月次）」と対比で覚えるとDが一瞬で浮かぶ
「日次評価・継続的な流通市場での取引が必須」は代替投資には当てはまりません。多くの代替投資（PEファンド、プライベートデット、不動産など）は流動性が低く、評価も頻度が低い（四半期・月次）。A（高集中）、B（低流動性）、C（レバレッジ・空売り・デリバティブの活用）は代替投資の典型的な特徴です。本問はleast likely型。

Q002 · L1-1.2.3-004

Case Study 7. A CAIA member is a consultant to a large pension fund. A hedge fund manager who is currently being evaluated for a potential allocation sends the consultant an expensive gift and an offer of a paid trip to an exclusive resort during the evaluation period. Which of the following best describes the appropriate response under the CAIA ethical principles?
  • . Accept the gift but decline the trip, because the gift is a customary business courtesy.
  • . Accept both, but disclose them in a post-award report after the allocation decision is made.
  • . Accept both and keep them confidential to avoid embarrassment for the fund manager.
  • . Decline both and notify the appropriate parties, because accepting them during an active evaluation creates a conflict that could compromise objectivity.
正解: D
🎯 秒殺テク
- 評価中の高額贈答・接待→「辞退＋報告」するDが正解 - A（慣例と軽視）、B（決定後開示では手遅れ）、C（非開示）はすべて客観性を損なう→消去 - 贈答・接待問題は「断る」選択肢がほぼ正解。「慣例だからOK」は高額贈答には当てはまらない
評価（割当決定）の最中に送られる高額な贈答・接待は、客観性を損ない得る利益相反を生じさせます。CAIAの原則では、そのような恩恵を辞退し、必要に応じて適切な関係者に報告することが求められます。Aの「慣習的なビジネス儀礼」は高額な贈答には当てはまらない。Bの決定後開示では評価の公平性を既に損なっている。Cの非開示は利益相反の管理として不適切です。

Q003 · L1-1.2.4-002

A newly hired CAIA member is asked to review the firm's compliance program. Which of the following elements is most central to an effective ethics and compliance program under the CAIA framework?
  • . A policy of never recording investment decisions to preserve flexibility.
  • . A single annual certification signed by the CEO, with no other controls.
  • . Periodic legal opinions confirming that no lawsuits have been filed against the firm.
  • . A documented code of conduct, ongoing training, monitoring and enforcement, and a mechanism for reporting potential violations.
正解: D
🎯 秒殺テク
- コンプライアンス体制は「複数の要素が揃った選択肢」が正解→Dの「code of conduct＋training＋monitoring＋reporting」のフルセット - A（記録しない）・B（年1回の証明書のみ）・C（訴訟不存在のみ）は単一要素で不十分→消去 - 「複数の仕組みが並んだ選択肢」を選ぶ。単一の安心材料だけの選択肢は不正解
効果的な倫理・コンプライアンス体制には、文書化された行動規範、継続的な研修、モニタリングと執行、違反報告の仕組みが不可欠です。Dが正解。Bの単年次証明書のみ、Cの訴訟不存在の確認のみでは不十分。Aの投資判断の記録を残さない方針は監査可能性を損ない、コンプライアンス違反につながります。

Q004 · L1-1.1.5-027

Which of the following is the most accurate statement regarding the distribution of returns for alternative investments?
  • . The return distributions of most alternative investments are approximately normal.
  • . Skewness and kurtosis are irrelevant to the performance evaluation of alternatives.
  • . Many alternative investment return distributions exhibit skewness and fat tails, and historical averages may be misleading.
  • . Alternative investments always exhibit negative skewness.
正解: C
🎯 秒殺テク
- 武器1「絶対表現」: A「approximately normal（ほぼ正規）」D「always exhibit negative skewness（常に負の歪度）」→誇張で消去 - B「skewness and kurtosis are irrelevant（無関係）」は統計の常識と逆→消去 - 残るC「歪度とファットテールがあり、歴史平均は誤解を招く」が正しい定番記述。代替投資＝正規分布から乖離、と暗記
代替投資のリターン分布は正規分布から乖離することが多く、歪度（skewness）と尖度（kurtosis、ファットテール）が顕著です。そのため平均値だけで評価すると誤解を招き、VaRなどのテールリスク指標や下方リスク（semivariance、Sortino比）なども併用する必要があります。A（正規近似）・B（無関係とする誤り）・D（「常に負の歪度」は過度な一般化で誤り）。

Q005 · L1-1.2.2-007

Case Study 3. A CAIA member managing a hedge fund allocation for a pension client is also a limited partner in a separate private equity fund that charges its investors higher fees than comparable funds. The member recommends that the pension client invest in that same private equity fund. The member discloses the relationship in the client's quarterly report but does not discuss the fee disadvantage in person. Which of the following statements best describes the member's behavior?
  • . The member fully satisfied his ethical duties because the relationship was disclosed in writing.
  • . The member breached the fair dealing principle because he failed to allocate the private equity fund opportunity to the pension client before his own investment.
  • . The member's disclosure was insufficient because he did not ensure the client understood the conflict and the fee disadvantage before acting on the recommendation.
  • . The member acted appropriately because personal investments are unrelated to professional recommendations.
正解: C
🎯 秒殺テク
- シナリオの欠点＝「書面開示だけで理解を確認していない」→Cの「insufficient...did not ensure the client understood」がそのまま指摘 - A（書面で足りる）は過剰擁護、B（機会配分にすり替え）・D（無関係と断じる）は本質から逸れる→消去 - 問題文の「欠点」を正確に言い当てた選択肢が正解
利益相反の管理において、形式的な書面開示だけでは不十分です。CAIAの原則では、開示が「効果的」であり、クライアントがその意味（ここでは手数料の不利）を理解した上で判断できることが求められます。Cは、理解を伴わない開示では不十分とする点で正しい。Aは書面開示のみで足りるとする点が誤り。Bは機会配分の公平性の問題を混同しており、本件の主要な問題は開示の質です。Dは個人的投資が推奨と無関係という前提が誤りです。

According to the CAIA Association's enforcement approach, which of the following statements is most accurate?
  • . The CAIA Association has no mechanism to discipline members for unethical conduct.

Q007 · L1-1.2.2-006

Which of the following best describes the fiduciary duty of loyalty as it applies to an investment professional managing client assets?
  • . Loyalty requires the professional to put the interests of the beneficiaries first and to avoid conflicts of interest that could compromise that priority.
  • . Loyalty requires the professional to prioritize the interests of the firm that employs him over the interests of any individual client.
  • . Loyalty requires the professional to disclose all fee structures but imposes no obligation regarding investment selection.
  • . Loyalty is satisfied if the professional follows a written compliance manual, regardless of outcomes.
正解: A
🎯 秒殺テク
- 「loyalty＝クライアント（受益者）の利益を最優先＋利益相反回避」→Aが定義そのもの - B（雇用者優先）は方向が逆、C（投資選択に義務なし）は義務を否定、D（文書準拠で足りる）は過小評価→消去 - 定義問題は「クライアント優先」を含む選択肢が正解
受託者責任のうち忠実義務（duty of loyalty）は、受益者（クライアント）の利益を自己または雇用者の利益より優先し、その優先順位を損なう可能性のある利益相反を回避することを要求します。Aが忠実義務の核心を最も正確に述べています。Bは雇用者優先で誤り。Cは投資選択に関する義務を否定しており誤り。Dは文書準拠のみで足りるとする点が誤りです。

Q008 · L1-1.2.1-003

Case Study 8. A CAIA member is preparing the track record of a new commodity fund for an investor questionnaire. To make the track record appear more attractive, the member backfills the fund's returns using a strategy that was never actually traded before the fund's official launch date. Which CAIA principle does this behavior most directly violate?
  • . High standards of professional conduct, because the backfilled track record misrepresents the fund's actual performance history.
  • . The duty of loyalty to limited partners, because the backfill changes the fund's legal structure.
  • . Fair dealing, because the backfill benefits the fund's custodian.
  • . None, because backfilling is a common industry practice that is always acceptable.
正解: A
🎯 秒殺テク
- 実績の偽装（backfill）＝「実績を歪める」→Aの「misrepresents the fund's actual performance」が直接指摘 - B（法務構造）・C（カストディアン）は無関係、D（常に許容）は開き直り→消去 - 虚偽表示シナリオは「misrepresent」を含む選択肢が正解
実際には運用されていなかった戦略のリターンをローンチ前日付までさかのぼって（backfill）表示することは、実績履歴を歪め、投資家に誤った情報を提供するもので、高水準の専門的行為および誠実性の原則違反です。Aが正解。Bは法務構造の変更という誤った説明。Cはカストディアンとの関係で誤り。Dの「常に許容される」という主張は誤りで、backfillは開示と公正性が条件となる場合もありますが、未運用戦略の偽装は許されません。

Q009 · L1-1.2.1-006

Which of the following actions best demonstrates a CAIA member's commitment to maintaining high standards of professional competence?
  • . Delegating all complex valuation work to outside vendors without reviewing their methodology.
  • . Limiting professional advice to areas in which the member has sufficient knowledge and experience, while engaging in continuous education.
  • . Relying exclusively on the firm's legal team to make all investment decisions.
  • . Avoiding any new asset class once the member has qualified, to reduce risk of error.
正解: B
🎯 秒殺テク
- 「専門性を高める行動」が答え→Bの「sufficient knowledge and experience」＋「continuous education」がそのまま - A（外部委託に丸投げ）、C（判断放棄）、D（新規回避）は能力の行使・向上ではない→消去 - 委任・放棄・回避の選択肢はすべて不正解。「自分の範囲内で＋学び続ける」が正解

Q010 · L1-1.2.3-007

Case Study 6. A CAIA member is a senior executive at a company that is about to announce a major acquisition. While the information is still material and non-public, the member places a personal trade in a related publicly listed company that will likely benefit from the acquisition. Which of the following statements best describes the member's conduct?
  • . The member is in compliance because no law prohibits trading in a related company.
  • . The member is in compliance because the trade was personal and not made on behalf of a client.
  • . The member is in violation of the integrity and high standards of professional conduct principles, and the trade may also constitute illegal insider trading.
  • . The member is in violation only if the trade resulted in a personal profit.
正解: C
🎯 秒殺テク
- 重要非公開情報での取引＝インサイダー。Cの「in violation...may also constitute illegal insider trading」がそのまま答え - A（法律が禁止していない）、B（自己売買だからOK）、D（利益が出た時のみ）はすべて言い訳→消去 - 「非公開重要情報で取引」はどんな言い訳も免責にならない。自己売買・個人取引は言い訳にならない
重要かつ非公開な情報に基づく取引は、CAIAの誠実性・高水準の専門的行為の原則に反し、多くの法域でインサイダー取引として違法となり得ます。取引が「個人のもの」であることや「関連会社」であることは免責になりません。Cが正解。A・Bは取引主体や対象の言い訳で成り立たない。Dは損失の場合に免責されるという誤った考え方です。

The member is asked by the compliance officer whether the arrangement is acceptable under the CAIA ethical principles. The compliance officer states that the key issue is not the existence of the conflict itself, but how the firm manages it. Which of the following responses best reflects how the CAIA principles treat such conflicts?
  • . All commission arrangements are prohibited because any compensation linked to product sales creates a conflict.
  • . Conflicts are acceptable only if the member personally avoids ever recommending a sponsored product.
  • . Conflicts are acceptable as long as they are not disclosed, to avoid alarming clients.
  • . Unavoidable conflicts are acceptable as long as they are disclosed to clients and managed with appropriate safeguards such as transparency and strong governance.

- 利益相反は「排除」ではなく「開示＋管理」→「disclosed」「managed」「transparency」「governance」が揃うDが正解 - Aの「all...prohibited」・Bの「never」は絶対表現→ほぼ不正解。Cの「not disclosed」は常識と逆 - 「開示と適切な管理」という方向の選択肢を最優先で選ぶ

Q013 · L1-1.1.6-022

According to the CAIA curriculum, which of the following is most likely to be considered one of the "investor invariants" that hold for all rational investors?
  • . Investors always prefer investments with higher fees.
  • . Investors are indifferent to liquidity.
  • . Investors have a preference for higher expected return and a dislike of risk, other things being equal.
  • . Investors prefer less wealth to more wealth.
正解: C
🎯 秒殺テク
- キーワード一致: 「investor invariants（投資家の不変項）」＝合理的投資家なら誰でも持つ普遍的選好＝「高い期待リターンを好み、リスクを嫌う」→Cがそのまま - 消去法: A（高い手数料を好む）B（流動性に無関心）D（富を減らすのを好む）は人間の常識と真逆→消去 - 「不変項＝より多くの富を好む・リスクを嫌う・高リターンを好む」の3点セットで暗記
合理的投資家の「不変項（invariants）」とは、状況によらず成立する普遍的な選好で、(1) より多くの富を好む、(2) 他の条件が同じなら高い期待リターンを好む、(3) 他の条件が同じならリスクを嫌う、などが挙げられます。Dは逆（より多くの富を好む）、A・Bは合理的選好と矛盾します。

Q014 · L1-1.2.2-003

Which of the following best describes Rika's behavior under the CAIA ethical principles?

Q015 · L1-1.1.7-026

Compared with traditional public equity investments, alternative investments are generally characterized by:
  • . Higher transparency and more frequent independent valuation.
  • . Greater heterogeneity of strategies and less standardization of vehicles.
  • . Lower management fees and higher liquidity.
  • . Tighter regulatory reporting requirements across all jurisdictions.

  • . $2.38
